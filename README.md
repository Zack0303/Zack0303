## 🎓 Have a research idea, but don't know how to turn it into a proposal?

**[Research Navigator](https://github.com/Zack0303/research-navigator)** helps master's and PhD students narrow a topic, plan a study, and review a draft against documented programme requirements.

- **Start with what you have:** an idea, a draft, or an official requirements document.
- **Work in your preferred language:** explanations and submission language can be different.
- **See what needs attention:** a provisional direction, an evidence-status checklist, and concrete next actions.

**No coding background or Codex installation needed for the web-chat route.**

### Try a small example
1. [Open the web-chat instructions](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Zack0303/research-navigator/main/WEB-START.txt) and paste the whole text into a new AI chat, or upload it if supported.
2. Say: “I'm a history master's student interested in local newspapers. Help me narrow my topic. Explain in Spanish; my submission language is English.”
3. Use the first questions and provisional plan to decide your next step. No personal draft is needed to try it.
